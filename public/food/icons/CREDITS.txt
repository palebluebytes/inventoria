Rations icon
============

"anchovy tin" by Michael Senkow, from the Noun Project.
    https://thenounproject.com/icon/anchovy-tin-7864233/

Licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution License (CC BY 3.0).
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Changes: the black line art was scaled and centred on a white ground, and
re-rendered at 512, 192, 180 and 32 pixels square, plus a maskable variant with
the art re-laid inside Android's safe circle. The ground is kept only where the
drawing encloses it and cleared to transparency outside, except on the 180,
where it is an opaque disc, and on the maskable, where it fills the canvas. No
line was redrawn.

CC BY 3.0 clause 4(a) asks that the author, the title, the source and the
licence travel with every copy of the work, and that a derivative say it is
one. This file is that notice, and it ships to /food/icons/CREDITS.txt beside the
images it describes -- the same route public/fonts/OFL.txt takes for the
typeface. docs/icon-provenance.md carries the full derivation.
