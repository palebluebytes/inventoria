Inventoria face marks
=====================

Each face of the app carries a mark, and each mark is somebody's work. CC BY 3.0
clause 4(a) asks that the author, the title, the source and the licence travel
with every copy, and that a derivative say it is one. This file is that notice,
and it ships to /icons/CREDITS.txt beside the images it describes -- the same
route public/fonts/OFL.txt takes for the typeface and public/food/icons/CREDITS.txt
takes for the Rations install icons.

All eight are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution License (CC BY 3.0).
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

    "Triquetra" by Michael Senkow, from the Noun Project.
    https://thenounproject.com/icon/triquetra-73068/

Changes: in every case the black line art was scaled to 86% of a square canvas
and centred, and the ground was kept only where the drawing encloses it and
cleared to transparency outside. The face marks were then re-rendered at 256
pixels square. The Inventoria mark, which is also an install icon, was re-rendered
at 512, 192, 180 and 32 pixels square, plus a maskable variant with the art
re-laid inside Android's safe circle; on the 180 the ground is an opaque disc and
on the maskable it fills the canvas. No line was redrawn in any of them.

docs/icon-provenance.md carries the full derivation and the measurements.
